02 What I'm working on

ETS — UX / Product Designer. September 2025 to present.

I'm building an interactive web visualization that connects Calculus concepts to the Mechanical Engineering and Computer Science topics that use them. Students can filter and sort in real time, and every connection comes with a rationale, so the map explains itself instead of just listing links.

The goal is intuitive learning: seeing why a concept matters before being asked to master it.

03 Interests

I studied graphic and product design as an undergraduate, and design is still the thing my hands know how to do — now across spatial computing, XR and interaction. Stories are what I return to when nobody is assigning anything: reading manga and novels, writing my own, wanting to draw them.

Teaching is where those meet in public. I teach art and design to high school students at two schools, which means turning taste into something transferable. The overlap I care about is making something that can be felt — a story, a lesson, or a product — instead of just listed.

07 What I actually engage with

The pattern: The last stretch of likes splits in two. One side is animals used as comedy — kennel work versus the fantasy of petting puppies all day, a dog wearing whatever it stole, a cow mid-scream. The other is making as play: a hot dog turned into an octopus, goldfish swimming in drinking glasses. I am not engaging with lifestyle. 10 If I could only follow one account

影视飓风 · MediaStorm

If I could only follow one brand, it would be 影视飓风. They treat filmmaking as a craft you can learn in public — cameras, light, the whole pipeline — and then they built a company around that craft: a material library, a classroom, products. “Infinite Progress” is not a caption. It is a working method. That is the account I would keep: proof that making, teaching, and building a world can be the same job.
